## A single open map, not separate levels

The official game description promises "a spacious map with many buildings and roads to explore,"
and doesn't describe the game as split into separate levels or servers by area — it reads as one
continuous open world.

## Confirmed areas, from official screenshots

- **Downtown / "New City"** — an official promotional screenshot labeled "New City" shows a
  downtown district with high-rise buildings and multi-lane roads.
- **Suburban neighborhoods** — official screenshots show residential streets with houses, driveways
  and garages, plus at least one furnished house interior.
- **Highway tunnels** — official screenshots show multi-lane tunnel sections with dense traffic.

## The Mars easter egg

An official in-game badge named **"Welcome to Mars"** exists (awarded 410+ times as of this
writing), confirming a Mars-themed area or event is reachable somewhere on or off the main map. The
exact way to trigger it isn't documented anywhere official, so how to reach it is marked
`Unconfirmed`.

## Traffic lights

The developer's own update history (in the official group description) lists "Traffic lights
added" as a real update, meaning intersections on the map are governed by traffic signals that the
Autopilot/FSD system can detect — see [FSD & Autopilot](/fsd).

## Unconfirmed for now

A full named list of map districts, a map overview image, and the exact trigger for the Mars area
aren't published anywhere official. This page will be updated once a verifiable source is found.

## The official community: Plaid Studio

Autopilot Simulator is run through **Plaid Studio**, its official Roblox community group, which
has more than 3 million members as of this writing. The group is owned by the game's creator, whose
Roblox display name is **ModelS** (username **Jun4649s**, Roblox-verified). The group description
is also where the developers post update history and the game's own FAQ.

## Is there an official Discord?

**Not confirmed.** Searching for an official Discord server for this specific game did not turn up
a link posted by the developer or referenced on the game's own Roblox page or group description —
only the Roblox group above is confirmed as an official channel. If an official Discord exists and
gets found later, this page should be updated with the actual invite link rather than guessing one.

## Creators covering the game

A few YouTube channels focus specifically on Autopilot Simulator, based on public channel
descriptions and video content:

- **Erepma** — a creator whose channel is dedicated to the game, with tutorial-style videos (e.g.
  "How to enable dashcam in Autopilot Simulator," "Everything YOU need to know about Autopilot").
- **Autopilotblox** — another channel making content specifically about the game.
- Wider Roblox creators have also covered it, including a test/review video by **OMB Gaming** that
  had over 200,000 views as of research, and a multi-part FSD series by **Stormblazerplayz**.

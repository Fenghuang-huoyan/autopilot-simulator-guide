## The official community: Plaid Studio

Autopilot Simulator is run through **Plaid Studio**, its official Roblox community group, which
has more than 3 million members as of this writing. The group is owned by the game's creator, whose
Roblox display name is **ModelS** (username **Jun4649s**, Roblox-verified). The group description
is also where the developers post update history and the game's own FAQ.

## The official Discord

**Confirmed, corrected from an earlier version of this page.** The creator channel Erepma links to
it directly from its channel page, labeled "APS Offical discord server" — opening the invite shows
a real Discord server named **"Plaid Studio"** (matching the Roblox group's name exactly), with
roughly 386 members online and **4,988 total members** at the time we checked. Invite:
`discord.com/invite/fKnAhFrfsD`. We didn't join or read messages inside it — verification here is
limited to confirming the server itself exists, is named after the developer's own studio, and is
directly linked from the most dedicated creator's channel page.

## Creators covering the game

- **Erepma** (`@Erepma`) — the most dedicated channel: 684 videos, ~1,270 subscribers, channel
  description reads "Hello, im Erepma, a Roblox youtuber for the game Autopilot Simulator (APS)."
  Content spans tutorials, feature tests (e.g. "Testing the new AEB V3"), and an ongoing
  progression series ("APS Series: 0 to 300k Cash"). We watched two of these videos in full to
  source [Controls](/controls) and [FSD & Autopilot](/fsd) — most other claims about this channel
  are limited to what's visible on its public channel page (video counts, titles, descriptions),
  not full video content.
- **Autopilotblox** — another channel making content specifically about the game; not watched in
  depth for this site.
- Wider Roblox creators have also covered it, including a test/review video by **OMB Gaming** that
  had over 200,000 views as of research, and a multi-part FSD series by **Stormblazerplayz** —
  neither watched in depth for this site, so no specific claims from them appear elsewhere on
  this site.

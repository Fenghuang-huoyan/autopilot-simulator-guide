## 100+ vehicles, not just Tesla

The official game description states: "You can drive around on Autopilot or FSD with 100+ ICE,
Hybrid, and Electric vehicles." Despite the Tesla-inspired name and UI, the roster isn't
Tesla-exclusive — official promotional screenshots show a wide mix:

- **Tesla-styled models** — a Cybertruck-styled pickup and Model Y/Model 3-styled sedans appear
  repeatedly across official media; the in-game phone app itself labels one vehicle profile
  "ModelS."
- **Other electric and hybrid-styled vehicles** — additional EV-styled hatchbacks and crossovers
  appear in official "New Cars" promotional screenshots, distinct from the Tesla-styled models.
- **ICE-styled vehicles** — SUV and sedan body styles resembling mainstream gas-powered cars also
  appear in official screenshots (including a badge-style front end resembling a BMW-style grille).

In-game license plates follow an "APS-###" format, visible across official screenshots.

## Fastest car: unconfirmed

No official top-speed list or stat sheet is published for this game. Roblox doesn't expose vehicle
stats through its public API for this title, and there's no official in-game leaderboard for speed
that's been verified for this page. Community YouTube channels dedicated to the game (Erepma,
ding.gaming) have published test/review videos that could plausibly contain this kind of
comparison — but until a specific, dated video is checked and its claim verified, "fastest car" is
marked `Unconfirmed` rather than guessed.

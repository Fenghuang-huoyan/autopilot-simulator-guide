## No official keybind list exists

Autopilot Simulator doesn't publish a control-scheme page, and the game's third-party wiki
(previously on Fandom) no longer exists ("this wiki does not exist"). What's below is drawn
directly from the game's own official promotional screenshots, cross-checked where a specific
control is visibly labeled on screen. General movement (driving with WASD/arrow keys, the Roblox
default for vehicle games) is `Unconfirmed` for this specific title and not asserted here.

## The dashboard touchscreen

The car's dashboard has a center touchscreen styled after a real Tesla display, showing:

- Current speed and the posted speed limit
- A live map with your route
- An **Enable/Disable Self-Driving** toggle to hand control to Autopilot/FSD
- A dock of icons along the bottom, including **Select Media**, **Navigate**, **Home**, and
  **Spawn**

Once self-driving is engaged, the screen overlays a blue path visualization ahead of the car — the
same visual style real Tesla FSD uses to show its planned route.

## The phone app

A separate in-game phone UI (also modeled on Tesla's real companion app) is where most car
features live, based on official screenshots:

- **Control** — vehicle-specific controls menu (shown for a Cybertruck-styled vehicle in official
  media)
- **Alarm**
- **Flash**
- **Light Show** — see [FSD & Autopilot](/fsd) for what triggers it
- **Summon** — opens a screen with press-and-hold directional buttons to move the vehicle remotely,
  plus a **"Smart Summon (Beta)"** mode; the map preview in this menu is labeled "currently
  unavailable" in the official screenshot, so its exact behavior is `Unconfirmed`
- **Settings** — toggles for Dark Mode, Real Time Clock, Disable Lock Screen, Notifications and
  Wallpaper

## Unconfirmed for now

The exact keyboard/controller bindings for manual driving, camera controls, and any mobile-specific
touch controls aren't published anywhere official. If you find a verified source (official Roblox
Discord post, in-game tutorial text, etc.), this page should be updated to cite it directly rather
than guessed.

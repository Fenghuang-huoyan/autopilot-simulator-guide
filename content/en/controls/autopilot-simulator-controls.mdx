## No official keybind list exists

Autopilot Simulator doesn't publish a control-scheme page, and the game's third-party wiki
(previously on Fandom) no longer exists ("this wiki does not exist"). What's below is drawn from
the game's own official promotional screenshots plus two creator videos we watched and verified
frame-by-frame — not just their titles. General movement (driving with WASD/arrow keys, the Roblox
default for vehicle games) is still `Unconfirmed` for this specific title and not asserted here.

## Engaging Autopilot: it's a paid game pass

The creator video "The Full Self Driving in Roblox Autopilot Simulator is actually pretty good"
states directly in its description: "I'll be testing one part of a game pass in the game,
Autopilot Simulator." **Autopilot/FSD is gated behind a purchasable game pass**, not a default
free feature — this wasn't clear from the official screenshots alone. The on-screen prompt for
the toggle itself reads **"Autopilot / Tap To Start"** when off and **"Tap To Stop"** once
engaged — not the generic "Enable/Disable Self-Driving" label we'd assumed before actually
watching gameplay.

## The dashboard touchscreen

The car's dashboard has a center touchscreen styled after a real Tesla display, showing:

- Current speed and the posted speed limit ("Max")
- Battery percentage, a clock, and outdoor temperature (shown in °F)
- The player's display name
- A live map with a **"Navigate"** search box; in the video, the destination list showed a mix of
  generic stop types (Gas Station, Service Center, Camp) and named points of interest, plus a
  **"Save Home"** option and a **"Trip Cancel"** button once a route is active
- Once Autopilot is engaged, the screen overlays a blue path visualization ahead of the car

**What the creator's real-world test actually showed**: while Autopilot was engaged and
navigating, the video's own on-screen caption states "So it seems like Autopilot has almost ran
into an object" — a genuine near-miss during testing, not a polished demo. Separately, an
**"Detect Intersection — AutoTurn Available"** prompt appeared as the car approached a traffic
light intersection, confirming the intersection auto-turn feature actually triggers in play (see
[FSD & Autopilot](/fsd)).

## The Autopilot settings menu

A Shorts video titled "Testing the new AEB V3 in Roblox autopilot simulator" shows a dedicated
Autopilot settings screen with individually labeled toggles — this is a more complete and more
verifiable list than the one-line feature summary in the developer's group description:

- **Sentry Mode**
- **Regenerative Brake**
- **Auto Speed Limit**
- **Parking Sensor**
- **Auto Light**
- **Fold Mirrors**
- **Camera - Auto**
- **AEB** — the video's title itself confirms this feature has gone through at least one
  numbered revision ("AEB V3"), meaning it's actively maintained, not a one-off addition
- **FSD Visualization** — the blue path overlay described above
- **Drive Speed** and **Display Size** — settings-type toggles, exact effect `Unconfirmed`
- **Key** — labeled but its function wasn't shown on screen in this clip, `Unconfirmed`

**Sentry Mode** and **Regenerative Brake** in particular are real Tesla features neither the
official Roblox listing nor the developer's own group description mentions — they only surfaced
by actually watching this settings menu on screen.

## Drive modes and dashboard details, seen in a second creator's footage

A separate, larger creator video ("I tested AUTOPILOT on Roblox," 220K+ views) shows an even more
detailed control menu on a car labeled **"Model 3 Long Range AWD"** — again, actually watched, not
inferred from a thumbnail:

- **Drive Modes**: Acceleration can be set to **Sport / Standard / Chill**, and Regenerative
  Braking separately to **Standard / Low** — this is a more granular version of the single
  "Regenerative Brake" toggle listed above, or possibly a different menu for the same setting.
- A bottom dock in this menu offers **Setting**, **Wallpaper**, **Navigate**, **Spotify**, and
  **Drive Modes** — confirming a **Spotify integration icon** exists in-game, distinct from the
  generic "Music function" mentioned in [FSD & Autopilot](/fsd).
- The dashboard also shows **tire pressure readouts** (four values, e.g. "3.0 bar" each) — a
  TPMS-style display real Tesla dashboards also have.
- The **Summon** screen (see below) was also seen directly in this video: it's titled just
  "Summon," shows the car's live speed ("D - 0 MPH"), a small live map, press-and-hold up/down
  arrows captioned "Press and hold controls to move vehicle," and two buttons labeled **"Actually
  Smart Summon"** and **"Auto Steering"** — slightly different wording than the "Smart Summon
  (Beta)" label in the official screenshot referenced below, which may mean this UI has been
  updated since that screenshot was taken.

## The phone app

A separate in-game phone UI (also modeled on Tesla's real companion app) is where most car
features live, based on official screenshots:

- **Control** — vehicle-specific controls menu (shown for a Cybertruck-styled vehicle in official
  media)
- **Alarm**
- **Flash**
- **Light Show** — see [FSD & Autopilot](/fsd) for what triggers it
- **Summon** — opens a screen with press-and-hold directional buttons to move the vehicle remotely,
  plus a **"Smart Summon (Beta)"** mode; the map preview in this menu is labeled "currently
  unavailable" in the official screenshot, so its exact behavior is `Unconfirmed`
- **Settings** — toggles for Dark Mode, Real Time Clock, Disable Lock Screen, Notifications and
  Wallpaper

## Unconfirmed for now

The exact keyboard/controller bindings for manual driving, camera controls, and any mobile-specific
touch controls aren't published anywhere official. If you find a verified source (official Roblox
Discord post, in-game tutorial text, etc.), this page should be updated to cite it directly rather
than guessed.

## Where this list comes from

The game doesn't have a manual or patch-note page dedicated to FSD mechanics. This list is the
feature set the developers themselves state in the official Plaid Studio group description, which
reads: "You can experience a variety of functions such as lane change function, traffic light
detection function, intersection automatic turn function, summon function, auto park function, AEB
function, light show function, music function, etc."

## The features, one by one

- **Lane change** — the car changes lanes automatically while self-driving is engaged.
- **Traffic light detection** — the car recognizes traffic lights; the group's own update history
  separately confirms "Traffic lights added" as a real update, so this is an actively maintained
  system, not a one-off.
- **Intersection automatic turn** — the car can turn automatically at intersections without manual
  input.
- **Summon** — move the car remotely from outside it, via the phone app's Summon menu (see
  [Controls](/controls)), including a "Smart Summon (Beta)" mode.
- **Auto park** — the car parks itself.
- **AEB (Automatic Emergency Braking)** — the car brakes automatically to avoid a collision.
- **Light show** — a synchronized exterior light display, triggered from the "toy box" per the
  group's update history. This is confirmed by an official in-game badge named "You've started the
  light show!", awarded over 1.2 million times as of this writing.
- **Music function** — an in-car music system (exact controls not detailed in official sources).

## What the path visualization looks like

Once Autopilot/FSD is engaged, the dashboard touchscreen overlays a blue lane path ahead of the
car — visually modeled on real Tesla FSD's own path-prediction display. See
[Controls](/controls) for where this appears on screen.

## This is a simulation of the real thing, not the real thing

Autopilot Simulator borrows its name, UI style and feature list from real-world Tesla Autopilot
and Full Self-Driving software, but it is an independent, fan-made Roblox game — it is not made or
endorsed by Tesla, Inc. Nothing on this site should be read as a description of how real Tesla
vehicles behave.

## Unconfirmed for now

Numeric specifics — how far ahead AEB detects hazards, exact lane-change trigger conditions, how
reliable auto-park is — aren't published anywhere official and are marked `Unconfirmed` until a
verifiable source (developer post, or a specific, dated creator test video) turns up.

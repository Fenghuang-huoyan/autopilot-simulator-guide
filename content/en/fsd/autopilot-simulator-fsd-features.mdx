## Quick answer

Autopilot Simulator copies the idea of Tesla Autopilot / FSD inside Roblox. The developer group description, fetched October 23, 2026, still names lane change, traffic light detection, intersection automatic turn, summon, auto park, AEB, light show, and music. Creator videos add on-screen behavior. Those videos were not re-shot after the experience's Roblox `updated` time of September 25, 2026. Re-check the in-game Autopilot settings and the Store before you buy a pass.

## How to engage Autopilot / FSD

Exact labels can move. The experience `updated` time is September 25, 2026. Use this as a checklist against the live UI.

1. Enter a vehicle in Autopilot Simulator / FSD.
2. Open the Autopilot / FSD settings. That menu family is the one shown in the AEB V3 Short.
3. Enable only toggles that are actually on your screen.
4. In Erepma's March 11, 2025 video the control read **"Autopilot / Tap To Start"** when off and **"Tap To Stop"** when on. Those words describe that recording. Use the label your build shows.
5. Confirm the blue path on the dashboard touchscreen. If it is missing, Autopilot may be off, or the camera angle may hide the screen.
6. In that same March 11, 2025 footage, **"Detect Intersection — AutoTurn Available"** appeared near a signaled intersection.
7. AEB is not a guarantee. The March 11, 2025 caption says Autopilot almost ran into an object. Drop Autopilot before a risky maneuver.

## Path visualization

With Autopilot / FSD engaged, the dashboard touchscreen in the cited creator footage overlays a blue lane path ahead of the car. Official promo media also shows this FSD-style path. It is a UI homage, not documentation of a real Tesla. Placement notes are on [Controls](/controls/autopilot-simulator-controls).

## Check the Store before you buy a pass

Two separate facts, neither of which is a current price list:

- Erepma's March 11, 2025 video described testing part of a game pass. That does not prove every Autopilot function is paid in the current build.
- The group description fetched October 23, 2026 says a purchased game pass may take time to apply because of Wi-Fi or a Roblox server error. It does not name a price or which feature the pass unlocks.

Before paying:

1. Open the experience Store on Roblox.
2. Trigger the specific feature once and read any paywall prompt.
3. Buy only what that prompt names. Free tiers and prices are `Unconfirmed` on this page.

## This is a simulation

Autopilot Simulator is an independent Roblox game by Jun4649s / Plaid Studio. It is not made or endorsed by Tesla, Inc. Nothing here describes real Tesla Autopilot or Full Self-Driving on public roads.
